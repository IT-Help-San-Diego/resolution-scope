## 3. Decisions that are LOCKED (do not re-litigate)

- **Option B risk-weighted score** — two scores shown together, never one replacing the other:
  - **Coverage Score** = `present ÷ (present+absent)`, the *existing* number, unchanged and
    sealed under the current scheme.
  - **Risk-Weighted Score** (RWS) = derived view, normalized 0–100, version-tagged.
- **Identity-weighting (not state-weighting)** — Carey's ruling 2026-08-22. Each control has ONE
  fixed weight from its **absent-state** severity: DNSSEC/SPF/DKIM/DMARC/MTA-STS = 3;
  DANE/CAA/CDS = 1. A `Present` control earns its full identity weight; `Absent` contributes to
  the denominator only. The enforcement nuance (`p=reject` vs `p=quarantine` vs `p=none`) lives
  in the **severity label + finding narrative**, never a fractional weight. Rationale: state-
  weighting would fabricate "quarantine = 2.5" precision on a *deliberate-choice* axis — the
  CIA running `p=quarantine` to monitor attackers is *safe, differently*, not "83% as safe."
- **Seal separation** — the seal binds the 8 dispositions (raw measurements) only. RWS is
  derived FROM sealed data but is NOT itself sealed. Formula changes bump `SCORING_VERSION`
  (start 1), never `SEAL_SCHEME`. No migration, no re-seal, no flush.
- **Option B seL4** — verdicts cross the compartment boundary, validation does not. Accepted
  trust boundary (documented in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §7): the compartment trusts the IPC
  verdict channel. Option A (upstream hickory no_std DNSSEC) is long-term, unstarted.
- **CAA stays Low** — reality-checked: CAA is a note in unprotected DNS, can't stop a
  compromised CA (DigiNotar-class), and the real backstops are CT logs + CA/B mandatory
  checking. See spec §9 for the perception lesson (the CAA mirror of "DMARC p=none isn't done").

**One severity sub-decision still OPEN for Carey** (flagged in spec §5, separate from the
resolved identity-vs-state point): DANE (currently Low, arguably Medium — pins mail TLS) and
MTA-STS (currently High). The spec does NOT re-litigate severity; it derives weight from whatever
severity the code emits. If Carey later rules "DANE is Medium," the weight follows automatically.
